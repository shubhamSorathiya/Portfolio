Case Study 01

Cubescapes

Full Game Design Ownership for a Casual Block Puzzle Game

Cubescapes is a travel-themed, level-based block puzzle game designed around simple block puzzle satisfaction, deeper level objectives, obstacle variety, boosters, meta progression, and LiveOps support.

Genre: Casual Block Puzzle Platform: Android Role: Game Designer Team: Small Production Team

My Contribution

I contributed to the game from early planning to execution. My responsibilities included game design, feature planning, level design, FTUE, economy balancing, obstacle and booster design, LiveOps event design, documentation, internal testing, and coordination with artists, developers, animators, and stakeholders.

Design Challenge

The challenge was to make a familiar block puzzle experience feel deeper, more goal-oriented, and more replayable. The game needed to keep the genre simple and approachable while adding meaningful progression, level variety, and reasons to return.

Design Goal

  • Create a clear and fast FTUE.
  • Build level progression with challenge and relief.
  • Add long-term motivation through meta progression.
  • Use obstacles, boosters, and objectives to reduce repetition.
  • Support engagement through LiveOps events and rewards.

Design Process

  • Market study: Analyzed similar casual puzzle and block puzzle games to understand genre expectations, player hooks, level pacing, and opportunities for innovation.
  • Feature deconstruction: Studied how competitive features worked, then adapted the thinking to fit our own game goals instead of copying directly.
  • Documentation: Created GDDs, spreadsheets, wireframes, flow references, economy tables, and test cases to make the design clear for the team.
  • Level creation: Designed levels around player flow, goal clarity, obstacle interaction, booster opportunities, fail points, and relief after difficulty spikes.
  • Iteration: Reviewed levels through internal testing and analytics signals such as win rate, fail rate, repeated failure points, churn behavior, and resource spending.

Core Loop

Players start a level, place blocks, clear lines, complete objectives, earn rewards, spend progression resources in the meta, and return to the next level with a stronger sense of progress.

Level Design

Levels were planned with a difficulty curve that introduced mechanics gradually, created challenge peaks, and placed relief levels after harder moments to maintain player flow.

LiveOps: Rail Rush

A time-limited marathon-style event where players collected tokens through gameplay, completed event stages, and earned rewards. Temporary token boosts encouraged longer sessions.

NDA-Safe Impact

Exact internal data and performance metrics are omitted for confidentiality. The design work supported a clearer player journey, stronger level variety, more meaningful progression, improved repeat-play motivation, and better LiveOps readiness.
