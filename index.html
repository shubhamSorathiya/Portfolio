About me

Player-first design with production-ready execution.

I am a junior game designer focused on casual and puzzle mobile games. My design approach starts with the player experience: how players learn, where they feel challenged, what motivates them to continue, and how systems can make the game feel fair, satisfying, and rewarding.

I have worked with small teams on shipped mobile games, contributing to levels, core loops, FTUE, LiveOps, economy, feature documentation, analytics thinking, testing, and team coordination.
